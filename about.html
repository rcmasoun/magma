MAGMA SUPPLY

Magma Supply, established in 2015, is a premier manufacturer specializing in a wide range of construction and industrial products. We take pride in our extensive expertise and commitment to quality, ensuring that each product under our renowned brand names — Fencefit, Formfit, and Aquafit — meets the highest standards of durability, performance, and reliability.

Our comprehensive product line encompasses various categories tailored to address the specific requirements of different industries. Whether you're in the construction, precast concrete, water works, or traffic control industry, Magma Supply has the perfect solutions for you — from precast concrete joint mold rings and TMT frames to manhole ladder rungs, drainage systems, and underground chamber vault covers.

WHAT WE OFFER

Concrete Precast Joint Mold Rings, TMT Frames/Rings, Concrete Reinforcement Wire & Mesh, Lid Handles, Manhole Ladder Rungs & Setting Pins, Jacket Lock Assemblies, Drainage Wall Head Grills & Railings, Underground Chamber Vault Covers, and T-Bars for Trench applications. We also offer in-house customization for tailored precast concrete and drainage system solutions to suit any project specification.

QUALITY YOU CAN TRUST

Every product we manufacture is built to withstand demanding field conditions, from precise MS steel casting and hot-dip galvanized finishes to precision-machined components — backed by our commitment to durability, performance, and reliability across every brand we offer.
