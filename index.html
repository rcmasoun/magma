About Us

Magma Supply, Established in 2015, is a premier manufacturer specializing in a wide range of construction and industrial products. We take pride in our extensive expertise and commitment to quality, ensuring that each product under our renowned brand names—Fencefit, Formfit, and Aquafit—meets the highest standards of durability, performance, and reliability. Our comprehensive product line encompasses various categories tailored to address the specific requirements of different industries. Whether you're in the construction, precast, water works, or traffic control industry, Magma Supply has the perfect solutions for you. For robust security and delineation solutions, explore our chain link fence products. Designed to withstand harsh conditions, these products provide unparalleled security while maintaining an aesthetic appeal. If you're involved in concrete pouring, our concrete formwork accessories are a must-have.
